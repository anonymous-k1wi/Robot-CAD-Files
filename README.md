# Robot CAD Files
File archive for publicly shared robot designs.  These are intended to serve as reference CAD examples and should not be expected to be ready-to-go solutions for your needs.  I cannot promise to provide support or troubleshooting advice in any capacity.

If you do choose to reproduce these designs, use common sense and follow best safety practices for robotics.  You are responsible for your own safety and the safety of people and property around you.  You are also asked to be courteous and not register your robot for a competition under an identical name as the original.
